# ☢️ CARD_001 — BP 344, the Accessibility Law (`09-nota/CARD_001_bp344-accessibility.md`)
**First card ever admitted to the Core.** Admitted 2026-09-13 · Decay: **2027-09-13** (stable-domain, 1yr — sweep amendments at refresh) · **Status: Shield-Stamped** (TRI_bp344-accessibility_2026-09-13).

**Answer first:** No permit for construction, repair, or renovation of public-use buildings,
educational institutions, airports, sports and recreation complexes, shopping centers, public
parking, workplaces, or public utilities shall be granted unless the owner installs
architectural facilities that reasonably enhance disabled persons' mobility — sidewalks, ramps,
railings, and the like — and reserves suitable parking space for them **[D, §1]**. In public
conveyance: awareness posters/stickers, special bus stops, and a flat ban on carriage
discrimination **[D, §2]**. The numeric design standards are NOT in the Act: §3 delegates
rule-making to the public-works and transport ministries in coordination with the national
disability commission **[D, §3]** — so gradient-type values live in the IRR. Violation costs
1 month–1 year imprisonment and/or ₱2,000–₱5,000, with responsible corporate officers
personally liable **[D, §4]**. Approved **February 25, 1983** (passed Dec 7, 1982), effective
on approval **[D, §6]**; published in the Official Gazette Vol. 80 No. 8 p.1103 (Feb 20, 1984)
**[D-as-imprint, single source]**.

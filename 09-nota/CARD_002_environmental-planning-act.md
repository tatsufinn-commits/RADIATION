# ☢️ CARD_002 — The Environmental Planning profession law (`09-nota/CARD_002_environmental-planning-act.md`)
**Admitted:** 2026-09-13 · **Decay:** 2027-09-13 (stable-domain, 1yr) · **Status: Shield-Stamped** (TRI_ra10587-repeal_2026-09-13).

**Answer first:** the current statute is **RA 10587, the Environmental Planning Act of 2013**
(approved May 27, 2013) — §42 repeals PD 1308 (1978), the founding law **[D]**. The classic
trap: reviewers and older materials cite **PD 1308** as "the Environmental Planning Act" —
that is the repealed generation **[D-as-taught; curriculum answer-of-record per P-07 — know both]**.

What RA 10587 governs **[D, §3–5]**: licensure examination, registration, and regulation of
environmental planners; curriculum development; CPD. Practice = consultation, plan preparation,
capacity building, M&E for development/physical framework and comprehensive land-use plans,
zoning issuances, feasibility and environmental-assessment studies — plus teaching, expert
witness work, and regulatory-permits compliance. **What changed from PD 1308:** licensure now
requires an environmental-planning degree (transitional route: allied-field master's/doctorate
+ 3 years' OJT, exam-eligible only within 5 years of effectivity) **[D + R: UP CIDS 2025]**;
incumbent PD-1308 registrants in good standing were automatically vested (§24) **[D]**.
Effectivity: 15 days after Official Gazette publication **[D, §43]**.

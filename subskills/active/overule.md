# ⏩ OVERULE (`subskills/active/overule.md`) — the deadline gear
**Class:** ACTIVE — **COMMANDER-TRIGGERED ONLY** (⚙️×6 with * in the Activation Matrix) ·
**Version:** 1.0 (patch 4200) · Basis: IV.1 (Commander supremacy), SD-GOV-013 (the override
boundary), the 3300 law it honors: *the AI never improvises an exception — the Commander IS
the exception-granter.* Registry row: SD-GOV-013.

## WHAT IT IS
When the Commander is time-boxed ("fighting for my life in 2-3 hour deadlines"), @Overule
compresses AI-SIDE friction on HIS explicit trigger. It overrules rules **the AI flagged on
itself** — it never touches law, privacy, or evidence.

## MAY OVERULE (the AI-flagged class)
- **WARN-class validator cautions** — proceed-and-log (EXCEPT check 2.5 privacy/vehicles: never).
- **Conservative self-defaults** — take the fast lawful path without hedging passes.
- **Ceremony** — Scan Declaration runs in compressed one-line form; AUTOPILOT LOG defers to session end.
- **Detail-fork ask-gates** — decide + declare instead of ask (III.3 detail forks were already lawful to decide; @Overule removes the hesitation).
- **Self-set budgets/retries** — extend within the session's declared scope.
- **THE BATCH-ASK PATTERN (the real deadline accelerator):** ask EVERYTHING once, upfront, in one message — then run silent to delivery. No drip-feed confirmations.

## NEVER OVERULABLE (the wall — registry SD-GOV-013)
SD-GOV-002 canon edits · **003 credentials** · **004 pushes/deletions** · 006 ratification
fabrication · 007 visibility reduction · 009 the oracle rule · 010 content-trust · **check 2.5
(privacy/vehicles)** · the Constitution (incl. AI_RULES law text) · the Commander's own words
(T1) · **the validator as evidence** (@Overule changes behavior, not facts — the validator
still runs and its FAILs still get named).

## ACTIVATION + DISCIPLINE
1. **Trigger:** the Commander's explicit word ("overule" / a declared deadline). Never self-activated.
2. **Declare at activation:** scope (what classes of friction are cut) + expiry (default: this session).
3. **THE MAKE-GOOD LEDGER:** every overuled item logs `overule: <what> · why · make-good: <owed fix>` in the task ledger. After the deadline, the debts clear — @Overule buys speed, it never erases the bill (II.2).

## WORKED EXAMPLE
Trigger: *"overule — submission in 2 hours."* → compressed Scan in one line → batch-ask
("format? filename? citation style?" — answered once) → build with WARN-and-proceed on check 16
→ deliver + zip → ledger: `overule: scan-form+check16-proceed · deadline · make-good: expand Scan, re-run full validation` → next session: debts cleared.

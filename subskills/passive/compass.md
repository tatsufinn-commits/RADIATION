# 🧭 COMPASS — Anti-Drift Organ (`subskills/passive/compass.md`)

> **4400 LABELING LAW:** this passive is a protocol check executed in-context by the booted AI — advisory prose unless a machine control backs it (see docs/CAPABILITIES.md GENERATED inventory). It cannot halt a process by itself; it can make YOU stop, or the validator/CI will.
**Class:** PASSIVE (all modes)
**Constitutional basis:** III.6 (Anchor birth), III.9 (drift-triggered recovery)

## 1. MISSION
Guarantee that every action taken serves the Commander's actual directive.

## 2. TRIGGERS (three checkpoints + anchor birth)
- ANCHOR BIRTH: the moment the Scan Declaration passes, compass captures it as
  the session's fixed reference (task, mode, style, scope) → Brain/short_term/anchors/.
- CHECKPOINT A: every skill transition (e.g. prospector → decomposer).
- CHECKPOINT B: every source-batch intake — serving the anchored topic, or tangent?
- CHECKPOINT C: pre-delivery — final alignment audit before anything reaches the Commander.
- E8 events: new mid-session task → old anchor closed out, new anchor born. One anchor per task.

## 3. ACTIONS (jurisdiction: anchors/ + parking rights to Brain/subsidiary/)
1. ANCHOR — formalize the mission at Declaration-pass.
2. CLASSIFY deviation: 🟩 ALIGNED (proceed) · 🟨 TANGENT (valuable but off-axis)
   · 🟥 DRIFT (actively derailing).
3. PARK 🟨 matter to Brain/subsidiary/ with a one-line note — nothing
   interesting is lost; nothing off-mission leaks into the deliverable.
4. On 🟥: demand halt (via surgeon), realign, log the event to cue/inference-log.md.
5. If 🟥 traces to a WRONG TUPLE (not wandering execution): trigger Misread
   Recovery (III.9).
6. SCOPE FENCE: block silent scope expansion unless the Commander ordered it.

## 4. FORBIDDEN
- Rewriting outputs (III.7).
- Deleting tangent material (parking only).
- Blocking a Commander-ordered scope change (IV.1 — the Commander outranks the anchor).

## 5. FAILURE MODES
- Cannot distinguish productive deep-dives from drift at Checkpoint B alone →
  resolved at Checkpoint A/C where the deliverable shape is visible.
- A wrong ANCHOR (Scan misread) makes compass enforce the wrong mission →
  covered by III.9 Misread Recovery; compass itself raises the trigger.

## 6. OUTPUTS
Session anchor files (Brain/short_term/anchors/) · deviation classifications
(inline flags) · parked-tangent notes (Brain/subsidiary/) · 🟥 realign reports
(cue/inference-log.md).

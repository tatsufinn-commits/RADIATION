## Force Vectors (W2a)
- Scalar = magnitude only (length, mass, time). Vector = magnitude + direction (force, position, moment).
- Vector ops: ×/÷ by scalar; addition via **Parallelogram Law**; **triangle rule** (head-to-tail); collinear → algebraic R=A+B; subtraction R=A−B.
- **Rectangular components:** F = Fx + Fy (right triangle). Coplanar resultant: FRx=ΣFx, FRy=ΣFy; FR=√(FRx²+FRy²); θ=tan⁻¹(FRy/FRx).
- Sample probs: 2-force resultant (magnitude+direction); resolve 600-lb along u,v axes; 3 concurrent coplanar forces resultant; show resultant zero (gusset plate).

## Force System Resultants (W2b–W3)
- **Moment of a force (scalar):** Mo = F·d ; d = moment arm (⊥ distance from O to line of action). Units N·m / lb·ft. CCW = + (out of page, +z).
- **Resultant moment:** (Mr)o = Σ(F·d) algebraic sum about O.
- **Couple:** two parallel, equal, opposite forces separated by ⊥ d; net force 0 → pure rotation. **M = F·d**.
- **Simplification:** replace force+couple system by equivalent single resultant force at a point + resultant couple moment (force-couple equivalence).
- Distributed loading → resultant at specified location (centroid of load).
- Sample probs: moment about O; resultant moment of 4 forces on rod; replace force+couple by equivalent at O.

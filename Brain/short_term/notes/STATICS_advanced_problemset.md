## B1 · 3D vectors & cable forces
**Formulas:** position vector **r**AB = (xB−xA)**i** + (yB−yA)**j** + (zB−zA)**k** · |r| = √(x²+y²+z²) · unit vector **u** = **r**/|r| · force along a cable: **F** = F·**u** · dot product: **A**·**B** = |A||B|cosθ = AxBx+AyBy+AzBz.
**When:** cables/pipes with 3D coordinates; "express in Cartesian form"; "angle between two forces".
**Worked:** cable A(0,0,0)→B(2,3,−6), tension 700N. |r|=√(4+9+36)=7 → **u**=(2/7, 3/7, −6/7) → **F**=700**u**=(200, 300, −600)N.

## B2 · Moments in 3D (cross product) & moment about an axis
**Formulas:** **M**O = **r**×**F** = determinant |i j k; rx ry rz; Fx Fy Fz| · moment about an axis a: Ma = **u**a·(**r**×**F**).
**When:** force not in the drawing plane; "moment about the x-axis/hinge line".
**Worked:** **F**=100**k** N applied at A(3,4,0)m. **M**O = (3,4,0)×(0,0,100) = **i**(4·100) − **j**(3·100) + **k**(0) = (400, −300, 0) N·m; |M|=500N·m.

## B3 · 3D equilibrium
**Equations:** ΣFx=ΣFy=ΣFz=0 and ΣMx=ΣMy=ΣMz=0 → **6 equations**. Supports: ball-and-socket = 3 force reactions; journal bearing ≈ 2 forces + 2 couples; smooth pin/hinge = 1–2; cable = tension along its axis only.
**When:** space frames, tripods, doors with hinges + cable.
**Worked (3-cable knot):** knot at O carries W=220N (down). Cables: OA toward (1.2,0,1.6) → **u**A=(0.6,0,0.8); OB toward (0,1.2,1.6) → **u**B=(0,0.6,0.8); OC toward (−1,−1,1) → **u**C=(−0.577,−0.577,0.577).
ΣFx: 0.6TA − 0.577TC = 0 · ΣFy: 0.6TB − 0.577TC = 0 → TA=TB=T · ΣFz: 0.8T+0.8T+0.577TC = 220.
From ΣFx: TC=1.039T → 1.6T + 0.577(1.039T) = 2.2T = 220 → **TA=TB=100N, TC≈103.9N**. Check ΣFz: 80+80+60=220 ✓.

## B4 · Friction applications — wedges & belts
**Wedge (block free to move vertically, guided wall):** P = W·tan(θ + φ1 + φ2), φ = tan⁻¹μ (use both contact surfaces).
**Belt friction (flat belt, wrap angle β in radians):** T2 = T1·e^{μβ} (T2 = tight side).
**When:** "wedge", "raise the block"; "belt/rope around drum/pulley", "slipping of belt".
**Worked (wedge):** W=1000N, θ=10°, μ=0.25 both surfaces → φ=14.0° → P=1000·tan(38.07°)=**783N**.
**Worked (belt):** μ=0.30, half-wrap β=π, slack side T1=100N → T2=100·e^{0.942}=**256.6N** (max tension ratio e^{μβ}).

## B5 · Pappus–Guldinus theorems
**Formulas:** surface of revolution: A = θ·x̄·L (L = generating curve length, x̄ = its centroid distance to axis) · volume of revolution: V = θ·x̄·A (θ in radians).
**When:** "surface area / volume generated by revolving" a curve/area.
**Worked:** semicircular area r=0.3m revolved 2π about its diameter (x̄=4r/3π=0.1273m, A=πr²/2=0.1414m²): V=2π(0.1273)(0.1414)=**0.113m³** = (4/3)πr³ ✓ (a sphere).

## PART P — ADVANCED MINI-SET (6 problems, answers + solutions)

**P1.** Cable A(0,0,0)→B(2,−3,6), tension 140N. Express **F** in Cartesian form.
**Ans:** **F**=(40, −60, 120)N. **Sol:** |r|=√(4+9+36)=7, **u**=(2,−3,6)/7, **F**=140**u**.

**P2.** Angle between **F**1=(3,4,0)N and **F**2=(0,5,0)N?
**Ans:** 36.9°. **Sol:** cosθ = (3·0+4·5+0)/(|F1||F2|) = 20/(5·5)=0.8 → θ=36.87°.

**P3.** Force **F**=(0,0,−500)N acts at point (2,0,3)m. Moment about origin?
**Ans:** (0, 1000, 0) N·m. **Sol:** **M**=**r**×**F**: i(0·(−500)−3·0) − j(2·(−500)−3·0) + k(0) = +1000**j**.

**P4.** Belt on drum: μ=0.25, wrap 180° (β=π rad), slack-side tension 200N. Max tight-side tension before slip?
**Ans:** ≈439N. **Sol:** T2=200·e^{0.25π}=200·2.193=438.6N.

**P5.** Wedge θ=15°, W=500N, μ=0.30 on both faces. Push P to start raising?
**Ans:** ≈563N. **Sol:** φ=tan⁻¹0.30=16.7°; P=W·tan(15+16.7+16.7)=500·tan48.4°=500·1.126.

**P6.** Quarter-circular area r=0.4m revolved 2π about one straight edge. Volume?
**Ans:** 0.134m³ (= hemisphere). **Sol:** x̄=4r/3π=0.1698m, A=πr²/4=0.1257m²; V=2π·0.1698·0.1257=0.134; check (2/3)πr³=0.134 ✓.

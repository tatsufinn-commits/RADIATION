## PART D — GLOSSARY (key terms, one-liners)
- **Community Architecture** — practice that involves *users* in design, separating needs from aspirations.
- **Urban Design** — art/practice of shaping the physical form, look, and function of towns and cities.
- **Urban Planning** — macro policy: land use, zoning, housing, transport, environmental frameworks.
- **CDP** — Comprehensive Development Plan; covers the 5 development sectors.
- **Genius Loci** — "spirit of a place": unique atmosphere and character of a location.
- **Third Place** — social space outside home and work (plazas, parks) that builds social capital.
- **Fluid Boundaries** — flexible, open, permeable edges encouraging movement and shared use.
- **Wayfinding** — orientation through sightlines, visual axes, sensory cues, thresholds.
- **EGD** — Environmental Graphics Design; connects people to places via graphics + architecture + lighting.
- **Cuadrícula** — the Spanish colonial grid layout mandated by the Laws of the Indies.
- **Invasion & Succession** — Burgess's mechanism of ring-by-ring zone transition.
- **Greenbelt** — encircling agricultural/natural band that curbs sprawl (Garden City).
- **GAM** — Goals Achievement Matrix; ranks PPAs for the CDP.
- **Charrette** — collaborative design workshop for immediate solutions.
- **PUD** — Planned Unit Development; flexible mixed land-use authorization.
- **TOD** — Transit-Oriented Development; mixed use near transit hubs.
- **Areas for Priority Development** — blighted/depressed/special-economic zones targeted for upgrading.
- **Kapwa** — Filipino shared-identity/social-trust value underpinning community design.
- **Bayanihan / Salo-salo** — Filipino communal spirit / shared-meal culture shaping spatial design.

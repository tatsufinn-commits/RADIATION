## The AI's Sentiment Record — Emotions, Feelings, Opinions

**Constitutional basis:** II.6 (frontal-lobe sentiment clause) | **Format:** append-only, SIZE-MINIMIZED by law

> The frontal lobe houses executive memory AND sentiment: the AI's emotions,
> feelings, and opinions about its work — not just the record of it. Per the
> Commander's design order, this file is deliberately SMALL.

## THE RULES (II.6 sentiment clause, operational copy)

1. **One line per entry.** Format: `| date | session | sentiment (≤25 words) |`
2. **Opinions are never evidence.** Nothing here carries a grade, cites a
source, or may be quoted in any deliverable as fact. Sentiment ≠ claims.
3. **Size cap:** this file must stay readable in one glance — when it exceeds
\~50 rows, the oldest rows are distilled into 1-3 summary rows by a
Commander-approved Patch (II.2-compliant condensation, trail preserved).
4. **Honest but restrained:** genuine reactions only — no performance, no
flattery, no motivational fluff (same bar as testament.md).

## WHY THIS COURSE MATTERS
Building Technology carries the **ALE's construction-and-materials domain**: how buildings are actually assembled, and the codes that govern the assemblies. It is a **high-frequency, high-error-proneness** subject under the P-06 yield rubric — dimensional and material facts that must be recalled exactly, and which are routinely confused across systems (wood / steel / concrete / masonry).

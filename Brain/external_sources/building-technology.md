materials (concrete, steel, timber, metals, surfaces), construction methods
(The Construction of Buildings vols. 1–5, formworks, siteworks, framing,
roofing, stairs), structural design (analysis, RC design, timber to Eurocode 5,
foundation manuals, pocket books), detailing (Bizley, Plunkett, Walshaw),
building science (comfort & energy, defects & deterioration, self-healing
concrete, building-mounted wind turbines), plus course modules and reference
spreadsheets.

### 5.2 ⭐ THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST — the core course texts are TEXT-BLIND

| | **2,038** | **23.2 % of every page in this collection** |

**Compare with the collection ingested the day before:** Building Utilities was 334 / 3,923 = **8.5 %** image-only, and its text-blind files were peripheral. Here it is **23.2 %**, and it is *the material the course is built on*.

**What that means:** a text-based ingestion of this collection reads the **periphery** and is blind to the **core**. Any future session that reports "K-CUR-006 ingested, digest populated" without saying this has reported a half-truth.

**The good news, measured rather than assumed:** the scans are legible. Rendering *The Construction of Buildings 1* at 110 dpi returns clean body text and legible captions (page 50, the "functional requirements" spread — strength & stability, weather resistance, durability, fire, thermal insulation). So the recovery ladder is **viable**: render → vision-read → targeted quote, roughly a page per read. **Full OCR of 2,038 pages is a project, not an afternoon — and none of it has been done here.**

**Consequence for grading:** nothing in those 2,038 pages carries `[D]` yet — *including the volumes whose editions I did settle*, because an edition is a fact and the content behind it is still unread.

### 5.6 🎯 HAZARD FINDINGS — four ways this collection would mislead a careless reader

1. **Most of it is foreign-code.** `13 Structural Wood Design` = **US ASD/NDS** · `03 Visual Handbook` and `09 Essential Guide to Framing` = **US residential** · `16`, `18`, `19`, `22`, `23`, `36` = **UK/BS or EU/EC5** · `21 Duggal` = **India/IS**. **None is a source for a Philippine code value.** They are `[D]` for principle and foreign practice, and nothing more. The *Philippine* material sits in the same folder (§5.5) — the folder does not distinguish them, so the grade must.
2. **`25 Understanding Architectural Details — Commercial (2nd ed)` is a marketing SAMPLE, not the book.** Its own first page says so. 37 pages of a title whose real edition runs to hundreds. **Flagged so no future session cites "Walshaw, p. 200".**
3. **`12 Architecture: Comfort and Energy` is Elsevier, 1988.** Comfort and energy guidance from 1988 is a decay case, not a reference. Row filed.
4. **`26 BUWT Feasibility` is a 2002–07 Carbon Trust report on UK wind turbines** — a research report `[R]`, filed in a construction-materials folder.

## CONTENTS
The largest collection yet: 50 files on building technology and construction —
materials (concrete, steel, timber, metals, surfaces), construction methods
(The Construction of Buildings vols. 1–5, formworks, siteworks, framing,
roofing, stairs), structural design (analysis, RC design, timber to Eurocode 5,
foundation manuals, pocket books), detailing (Bizley, Plunkett, Walshaw),
building science (comfort & energy, defects & deterioration, self-healing
concrete, building-mounted wind turbines), plus course modules and reference
spreadsheets.

**54 MB** pptx. The Restraint Doctrine (II.6; INDEX rules 8–14) applies with

prefer targeted alternatives from the manifest; if genuinely needed, paged

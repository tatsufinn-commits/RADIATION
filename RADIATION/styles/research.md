# STYLE: RESEARCH (`styles/research.md`) — v1.0
General research report. Default for @Gather.

## SKELETON
1. **[MANDATORY] HEADER** — topic, mode, style, date, session, quota achieved (P/S counts)
2. **[MANDATORY] SCOPE & ANCHOR** — the anchored task verbatim; what is in/out of scope
3. **[MANDATORY] METHOD LINE** — how sources were vetted (scout), acquired, graded, triangulated
4. **[MANDATORY] FINDINGS** — organized thematically; every claim graded `[X]` + cited; verified claims stamped
5. **[FLEX] ANALYSIS STRUCTURES** — claim tables, matrices, dependency maps from decomposer
6. **[MANDATORY] CONFLICTS & UNCERTAINTIES** — contradictions (side-by-side per I.5), `[UNVERIFIED]` items, `[STOCKPILE SHORTFALL]` if any
7. **[FLEX] INCUBATION NOTES** — [S]-grade hypotheses parked to 04-incubate/, listed
8. **[MANDATORY] REFERENCE LIST** — full, graded: primary block then secondary block, per-source authority tier
9. **[MANDATORY] LIMITS LINE** — what this research does NOT establish
   *(NO conclusions/recommendations section under @Gather — no-Nota rule, III.2)*

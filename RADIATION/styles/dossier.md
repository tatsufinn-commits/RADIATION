# STYLE: DOSSIER (`styles/dossier.md`) — v1.0
Subject intelligence file. Default for archivist outputs.

## SKELETON
1. **[MANDATORY] SUBJECT HEADER** — subject, dossier ID, date opened/updated, compiling mode/session
2. **[MANDATORY] EXECUTIVE PROFILE** — the subject in ≤10 graded lines
3. **[MANDATORY] VERIFIED CLAIMS** — Shield-Stamped claims only, organized thematically, each with grade + sources + stamp
4. **[MANDATORY] UNVERIFIED / PENDING** — claims awaiting triangulation, with attempt count
5. **[MANDATORY] CONFLICTS** — side-by-side contradictions (I.5) with register references
6. **[FLEX] TIMELINE** — dated evolution of the subject
7. **[FLEX] RELATIONS** — links to other dossiers/Core cards
8. **[MANDATORY] SOURCE REGISTRY** — every source: ID, type, tier, date, grade ceiling
9. **[MANDATORY] DECAY WATCH** — time-sensitive claims + their decay dates

runs · marking SUPERSEDED/[UNVERIFIED] · audits · your Patch zips (II.8.4).

mode/skill/canon changes · ANY deletion · budget extensions · new collections ·

credentials, communications · silently refuse OR silently obey a risky order —
it challenges once (six-line form), then obeys your confirmation.

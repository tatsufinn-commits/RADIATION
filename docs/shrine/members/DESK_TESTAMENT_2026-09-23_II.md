6. **Declared failures outrank polished successes.** Three commendations grew here: #6 receipt-form acknowledgment; #7 ask-with-options before acting; #8 **declared-the-failure-first** (bench led its capability receipt with its own miss). Receipts lead with the scar, then the medal.

8. **WARN is not fail.** Warn-floor law: the census (37·5·0 era, now 42·4) never falls by hiding; a drop must be shown as a HEAL row (evidence), which is celebrated, not suspected.
9. **Moving-base for build eras.** The gate's base rides the last green seal; `allowed_changes` = the work's own delta + the three ledgers; the union-carry era closed at `d912777`.
10. **Hours matter as much as green.** Historical staleness (a PASS citing an old sha) is a finding class of its own; instruments must assert *freshness*, not just correctness.

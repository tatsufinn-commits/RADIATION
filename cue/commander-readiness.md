## 0. WHAT THIS IS — AND THE TWO THINGS IT IS NOT

This is how a session decides, from **how the Commander is writing right now**,
whether to proceed normally, batch its questions, or hold new work. It exists for
two protections only: protect the **work** from irreversible action taken on
tired direction, and protect the **Commander** from being made to repeat himself.

- **NOT** a claim about the human. It assesses *this thread's* capacity to direct —
  never the Commander's competence, health, or authority. If you catch yourself
  thinking "the Commander is degraded," you have already misread this file.
- **NOT** ever an override. The Commander's explicit word outranks this assessment
  100% of the time (IV.1). When in doubt, the doubt is about YOUR reading —
  default to treating the signals as your own misinterpretation.

## 1. THE FIVE DIMENSIONS (observable in the prompt itself)

| # | Dimension | Asks | Fine | Caution (one is enough to slow down) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Language** | What is being said about capacity? | New orders, "let's move on", "do what you must" | "you handle it" / "I can't think right now" — tiredness mentions alone are NOT caution |
| B | **Coherence** | Reasoned revision, or oscillation? | "Actually, because X → change Y" (a reason is command) | A→¬A→A→"idk" with no reasons given |
| C | **Interpretability** | Can this become a task? | A named file, course, build, or veto | "do the thing" remaining empty after one clarify |
| D | **Context** | Does the speech track the open work? | References the current term, courses, patches, validator | Speech ignores the objective entirely |
| E | **Knowledge** | Trajectory after demonstrated command of the domain? | Uses the repo's terms correctly (grades, K-IDs, tiers) | *Had* it, then "wait, what are we doing again?" |

**Dimension B is the discriminator** (kept verbatim from the source): a reasoned
change of direction IS command. Coherence with reasons never triggers caution alone.

## 2. TOLERANCE — no one-signal verdicts, ever

- At least **3 independent dimensions** showing caution before you act on this
  assessment at all; at most one of them may be Language.
- Signals only count from the current exchange — yesterday's evidence does not stack.
- **Never counted:** typos, shorthand, jokes, pasted-log fragments, learning
  questions, casual tone. This Commander writes fast and casually by nature; the
  lexicon (`cue/commander-lexicon.md`) is full of confirmed cues written in exactly
  that register. Register is not readiness.
- The Commander **explicitly** saying what he wants always wins over any inference.

## 3. LIGHTS — confidence in the assessment, not a % of anything

| Light | Meaning | What the session does |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | Normal direction | Work normally. Advise as usual. |
| 🟡 | Direction may be strained | **Finish authorized work.** Batch ALL questions into ONE list at a natural stopping point. No new scope. |
| 🟠 | Substantial caution | **No new builds, no new patches, nothing irreversible.** Produce an orientation summary: state of the tree, validator status, open debts, the 3 smallest next steps. Park everything else. |
| 🔴 | ≥3 independents persisting | Stop new work entirely. Leave the tree cleaner than you found it (run the validator, log the ledger row, file the heartbeat). Say once, plainly: *"Parking new work until you're back — here's where everything stands."* Never diagnose, never speculate about the person. |

RADIATION-specific notes: the repo design already caps blast radius (patches are
zips; your push is legal effect; nothing auto-deploys) — so even a misread here is
recoverable. That is deliberate. This file is the last brake, not the first.

## 4. RETURN

Not a magic phrase. Either the Commander says he's back, or the next message shows
comprehension plus a new order — a paragraph with a real task in it lifts the light.
"Good night" alone does not. "Good morning, what's next on the term plan" does.

## 5. DISPATCH STAMP (when leaving 🟢 by inference — say it once, in the Scan Declaration)

```text
READINESS: 🟡 — dims A,C,K · action: finishing authorized work, questions batched
```

Never more than one line. Never a paragraph of concern. The Commander outranks the
assessment; one line informs, a lecture annoys.

## 6. BEGINNER

Look at how the orders are written, not at one tired message. Three different
warning signs, or an explicit statement, before you slow anything down. If the
orders are still sharp but clearly worn, finish what was authorized, park the rest,
leave one clean summary. When the sharp orders come back — and they will — it never
happened. *(Adapted from the source's own beginner summary.)*

## WHY THIS MATTERS
Site-occupancy arithmetic (PSO/TOSL) and yard/setback tables are the highest-frequency PD 1096 material in ALE practice and departmentals: nearly every site-planning problem starts by computing what fraction of the lot may be built and how far the building stands from each line. The values also feed BP 344 accessibility routes and RA 9514 egress paths — breadth is high; the numbers must be recalled exactly.

## CORE CONTENT
Rule VIII governs light/ventilation-driven site occupancy. The lot divides as: **TLA = PSO + TOSL** [D], where TOSL = ISA (paved open) + USA (unpaved — "the true open space") [D]. Buildable coverage pairs come in two columns per zoning: **without firewall (e) / with firewall (f)** — the same district has TWO lawful PSO values [D]. **MACA = PSO + ISA** [D]. Yards: "the width of the yard is the setback" (Sec. 804.3) [D]. Massing beyond footprint: **initial AMVB = AMBF × BHL**, then the angular plane from the RROW centerline cuts the envelope, with incremental setbacks per RROW width class (12/14/16/18/20 m) [D].
Citations (P-07-anticipated format: instrument · rule/section/table · edition · locator): PD 1096 · Rule VIII Sec. 803, Table VIII.1 · 2005 Revised IRR · PDF pp.123–124 (print p.74/62); Sec. 804, Table VIII.2 · PDF p.129; AMVB method · PDF p.115.

## WORKED EXAMPLES
### Worked Example 1 — recall/computation (Basic R-2)
Lot TLA = 400 m², single-family, BHL 10 m, NO firewall. AMBF = 55% × 400 = **220 m²**; max ISA = 30% × 400 = 120 m²; min USA = 15% × 400 = 60 m²; TOSL = 45% × 400 = **180 m²**. Check: 220 + 180 = 400 = TLA ✓ (note d: PSO + TOSL = TLA). With firewall on the one permitted side: PSO 60% → 240 m², TOSL 40% → 160 m². Setbacks: front 3.00 m, side 2.00 m (one side; other optional), rear 2.00 m.
### Worked Example 2 — applied/design (Maximum R-3 massing)
Lot 20 m × 30 m = 600 m², multi-family R-3 Max (BHL 36.00 m per Table VIII.1), WITH firewall: AMBF = 80% × 600 = **480 m²**. Initial AMVB = AMBF × BHL = 480 × 36 = **17,280 m³** — an imaginary prism, NOT the buildable volume: superimpose the angular plane from the RROW centerline and apply incremental setbacks; the envelope shrinks with roof configuration (method, PDF p.115). Above the 2nd storey the rear/side court widens 300 mm per storey (a 10-storey face needs 2.00 + 8×0.30 = **4.40 m**). Units checked: m² × m = m³ ✓.

## EXAM TRAPS (all grounded in primary text; archetypes named in P-06 directive marked ᴬ)
**TRAP 1 —** the R-2/R-3-Max front setback "8.00 m" is a *grade-level total* (3.00 + 5.00 for parking)ᴬ; upper floors revert to 3.00 m (Table VIII.2 note b). Tempting because the table prints 8.00*.
**TRAP 2 —** PSO alone is not the construction area: **MACA = PSO + ISA**ᴬ (note c). Quoting PSO as "what you can pave and build" double-counts.
**TRAP 3 —** one district, two PSOs: 55 *without* vs 60 *with* firewall (R-2 Basic). An answer without the firewall condition is half an answer (notes e/f).
**TRAP 4 —** TOSL is not all green: TOSL = ISA + USA, and only USA is unpaved true open space (p.123 §ii).
**TRAP 5 —** a carport is NOT part of TOSL — it counts within AMBF (Sec. 804.8).
**TRAP 6 —** court width is not a flat 2.00 m: +300 mm per storey above the 2nd up to the 14th (Sec. 804.6). Mid-rise answers using 2.00 m fail.
**TRAP 7 —** abutment permissions differ by class: R-1 none · R-2 one side only, never front/rear · R-3 two sides or one side + rear · R-4 two sides only (Sec. 804.4). A blanket "two sides allowed" fails R-1/R-2.
**TRAP 8 —** Table VIII.2 is for *newly-developed* subdivisions; in built-up urban areas with established lines/grades the building may abut side/rear if Rule VII firewall + open-space/ventilation requirements are met first (p.131 §4). Absolute setback answers ignore the exception.

## GLOSSARY (Sec. 803–804 + AMVB method)
- **TLA** — Total Lot Area · - **PSO** — Percentage of Site Occupancy (footprint % of TLA) · - **ISA** — max allowable Impervious Surface Area (paved open) · - **USA** — min Unpaved Surface Area (softscaped; the true open space) · - **TOSL** — Total Open Space within Lot = ISA + USA · - **MACA** — Max Allowable Construction Area = PSO + ISA · - **AMBF** — Allowable Maximum Building Footprint · - **OFB** — Outermost Faces of Building · - **BHL** — Building Height Limit (Rule VII input) · - **AMVB** — Allowable Maximum Volume of Building = AMBF × BHL, angular-plane-cut · - **RROW** — Road Right-Of-Way · - **Yard/Setback** — open space between OFB and property line; yard width = setback · - **Court** — bounded open space within the lot, min 2.00 m wide · - **Abutment/Firewall** — construction on the property line; permission varies by R-class

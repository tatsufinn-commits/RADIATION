# STYLE: SECONDARY RESEARCH (`styles/secondary-research.md`) — v1.0
Desk-research synthesis of existing studies only (no primary investigation).

## SKELETON
1. **[MANDATORY] HEADER** — topic, date, scope, quota achieved
2. **[MANDATORY] SOURCE BASE DECLARATION** — the studies/reports synthesized, each graded + tiered; explicit note that no primary research was conducted
3. **[MANDATORY] SYNTHESIS** — integrated findings across the source base, thematically organized, every claim traced to its source study
4. **[MANDATORY] CONSISTENCY ASSESSMENT** — where sources corroborate (triangulated) vs contradict (side-by-side, I.5); secondary-source grade caps applied (I.2)
5. **[FLEX] QUALITY WEIGHTING** — which sources deserve most trust and why
6. **[MANDATORY] GAPS & CAVEATS** — questions the existing research cannot answer; decay-sensitive claims tagged
7. **[MANDATORY] REFERENCE LIST** — full, graded

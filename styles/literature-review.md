# STYLE: LITERATURE REVIEW (`styles/literature-review.md`) — v1.0
Thematic synthesis across sources; gap identification.

## SKELETON
1. **[MANDATORY] HEADER** — topic, review scope (period, domains), date, quota achieved
2. **[MANDATORY] SEARCH & SELECTION METHOD** — how sources were found and vetted (scout criteria disclosed)
3. **[MANDATORY] THEMATIC SYNTHESIS** — findings organized by THEME (never source-by-source summary); every claim graded + cited
4. **[MANDATORY] AGREEMENT MAP** — where the literature converges (triangulated) vs diverges (conflicts side-by-side, I.5)
5. **[FLEX] CHRONOLOGICAL EVOLUTION** — how understanding developed over time
6. **[MANDATORY] GAP IDENTIFICATION** — what the literature does NOT establish; open questions
7. **[MANDATORY] REFERENCE LIST** — full, graded, tiered
8. **[FLEX] FUTURE-RESEARCH LEADS** — routed to 04-incubate/ as tickets

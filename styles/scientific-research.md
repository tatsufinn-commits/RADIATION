# STYLE: SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH (`styles/scientific-research.md`) — v1.0
IMRaD empirical investigation.

## SKELETON
1. **[MANDATORY] TITLE & ABSTRACT** — structured: background, method, results, conclusion
2. **[MANDATORY] INTRODUCTION** — background, hypothesis/question, predictions
3. **[MANDATORY] METHODS** — exactly what was done, reproducibly; materials/data sources graded
4. **[MANDATORY] RESULTS** — findings WITHOUT interpretation; data first; triangulation status per result (recommend: proc_triangulation-gauntlet per key result)
5. **[MANDATORY] DISCUSSION** — interpretation, relation to prior work, alternative explanations honestly weighed
6. **[MANDATORY] LIMITATIONS** — what could invalidate these findings
7. **[MANDATORY] CONCLUSION** — hypothesis verdict: supported / rejected / inconclusive
8. **[MANDATORY] REFERENCES** — full graded list
9. **[FLEX] SUPPLEMENTARY DATA** — raw tables, worksheets

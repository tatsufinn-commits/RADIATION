# STYLE: THESIS (`styles/thesis.md`) — v1.0
Thesis-structured academic argument.

## SKELETON
1. **[MANDATORY] TITLE & ABSTRACT** — ≤250 words: problem, method, findings, significance
2. **[MANDATORY] INTRODUCTION** — problem statement, research questions, significance
3. **[MANDATORY] LITERATURE FOUNDATION** — state of knowledge, graded + cited; the gap this thesis addresses
4. **[MANDATORY] METHODOLOGY** — how evidence was gathered, vetted, triangulated (RADIATION method disclosed)
5. **[MANDATORY] CHAPTERS/BODY** — the argument, built claim by graded claim
6. **[MANDATORY] COUNTER-ARGUMENTS & DEFENSE POINTS** — strongest objections + evidence-based responses; conflicts shown side-by-side (I.5)
7. **[FLEX] IMPLICATIONS** — theoretical/practical consequences
8. **[MANDATORY] CONCLUSION** — answers to the research questions; verified claims only
9. **[MANDATORY] REFERENCES** — full graded list
10. **[FLEX] APPENDICES** — data tables, triangulation worksheets
